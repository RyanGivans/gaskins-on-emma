The Room

Old-school steakhouse soul. Downtown edge.

Lisa Provencio-Jones and Bert Jones brought the spirit of Gaskins Cabin Steakhouse to Emma Avenue, pairing signature prime rib, char-grilled steaks, fresh fish, poultry, pasta, and a polished lounge experience.

The result feels timeless instead of trendy: low golden light, a serious bar, and a room built for date nights, celebrations, work dinners, and “we should have done this sooner” evenings.
