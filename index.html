The dining room you didn't know you needed.

Gaskins on Emma brings dark, sophisticated energy to downtown Springdale. Everything here feels deliberate — from the black marble countertops to the warm amber pools of light cast by our pendant fixtures.

Our kitchen focuses on craft cooking and seasonal ingredients, delivering familiar American steakhouse classics with unexpected precision and bold, memorable flavors.

Whether it's a Tuesday evening cocktail or a milestone celebration, our dining room is built to be permanent. Not trendy. Just exactly what you want it to be.

Meet the Chef

Sam Walker

Chef Sam Walker brings an uncompromising dedication to technique and ingredient sourcing. His approach honors the steakhouse tradition while subtly breaking its rules, resulting in a menu that feels both timeless and urgently modern.
